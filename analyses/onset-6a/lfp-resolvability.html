LFP frequency and temporal resolution

1,820 area×frequency cells; 114 are temporally resolved. Whether independent estimators agree on when the change happened is itself the informative quantity here.

Unresolved does not mean unmodulated. Low-frequency LFP power can change strongly while its onset remains poorly localized in time. Response magnitude and temporal resolution are separate measurements, and this page reports only the second.

B4 · Censoring, and why there is no beta onset distribution

Beta latencies are strongly left-censored. Of 36 resolved beta cells, 17 sit exactly on the lower search bound and 25 (69%) fall at or below 20 ms. A naive beta onset distribution would report a bound, not a measurement, so none is shown here and boundary-pinned values are never rendered as ordinary latencies.
