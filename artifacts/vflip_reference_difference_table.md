# vFLIP Reference Difference Table (0.2.2-01)

## Executive Summary

This document establishes the authoritative reference comparison for `jnwb.laminar.vflip`,
`jnwb.laminar.vflip_from_lfp`, and `jnwb.laminar.label_layers` prior to implementation in `0.2.2-02`.
It compares three sources across all consequential operations:
1. **Contributed / Private Implementation**: `ae906a4:codes/functions/vflip2_mapping.py` (`vFLIP2`);
2. **Publication / Reference Algorithm**: Mendoza-Halliday et al. (2024) / Bastos et al. (2018);
3. **Proposed `jnwb.laminar` API**: Non-interactive, geometry-aware, unit-safe public library primitives.

Every difference is classified into one of:
- `IDENTICAL`: Identical mathematical formulation and execution.
- `EQUIVALENT`: Mathematically equivalent formulation with explicit derivation.
- `INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE`: Deliberate design change with explicit scientific/engineering justification.
- `UNRESOLVED`: Open ambiguity requiring maintainer resolution (must be empty to proceed).

## Authoritative Reference Table

| Operation / Dimension | Contributed Implementation (`vFLIP2`) | Publication / Reference (Mendoza-Halliday et al. 2024) | Proposed `jnwb.laminar` API | Classification | Mathematical Derivation / Justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Input Representation** | `data`: `(n_channels, n_freqs)` array with `DataType` (`"psd"`, `"power"`, `"amplitude"`); `area_labels` strings; `valid_channel_mask`; `bad_channel_mask`. | LFP power spectral density matrix across laminar depth contacts $c \in \{1, \dots, C\}$ and frequencies $f \in [f_{\min}, f_{\max}]$. | `psd: np.ndarray` `(n_channels, n_freqs)` in $(U_{\text{in}})^2/\mathrm{Hz}$; `freqs: np.ndarray` `(n_freqs,)` in $\mathrm{Hz}$; optional `ProbeGeometry`. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Decouples laminar estimation from downstream area taxonomies, condition labels, and manual strings. Explicit frequency coordinates (`freqs`) prevent bin-width scaling errors under arbitrary frequency resolutions. |
| **2. Frequency Selection** | Grid search over a hardcoded 4-row matrix (`SpectralProfile`: combinations of low 0–30 Hz and high 30–150 Hz). | Evaluates spectrolaminar contrast between alpha/beta (8–30 Hz) and gamma (50–150 Hz). | Explicit `band_low: Tuple[float, float]` and `band_high: Tuple[float, float]`, defaulting to canonical $(8.0, 30.0)$ and $(50.0, 150.0)\,\mathrm{Hz}$. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Eliminates unconstrained combinatorial grid search that overfits to specific recordings; gives callers transparent, explicit control over the physiological frequency bands under test. |
| **3. Spectral Normalization** | Columnwise z-score across channels per frequency: $\tilde{P}(c, f) = (P(c, f) - \mu_c(f)) / (\sigma_c(f) + \epsilon)$. | Relative power or depth z-score per frequency across contacts along the shaft. | Z-score standardization of power per frequency across contacts: $\tilde{P}(c, f) = (P(c, f) - \mu_c(f)) / \sigma_c(f)$. | **EQUIVALENT** | *Derivation*: For frequency $f_j$, standardizing across contacts removes 1/f broadband power scaling across frequencies: $\sum_c \tilde{P}(c, f_j) = 0$ and $\frac{1}{C}\sum_c \tilde{P}(c, f_j)^2 = 1$, weighting low and high frequency profiles equally. |
| **4. Spatial Ordering** | Assumes matrix rows $0 \dots C-1$ directly match linear physical order along probe shaft. | Assumes contacts ordered linearly from cortex surface (superficial) to deep (or vice versa). | Requires verified monotonic order via `ProbeGeometry.linear_order` or pre-sorted input array. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Eliminates assumptions about row order in unvalidated tables; integrates with `probe_geometry` to enforce verified physical layout along the shaft. |
| **5. Orientation** | Accepts `"superficial"`, `"deep"`, or `"both"`, mapped to $1, -1, 0$. Evaluates sign of goodness. | Superficially dominant gamma yields to deep-dominant alpha/beta; penetration angle or approach determines sign. | Accepts `orientation: str = "auto" \| "superficial_to_deep" \| "deep_to_superficial"`. Resolved in `VFlipResult.orientation`. | **EQUIVALENT** | *Derivation*: `"superficial_to_deep"` corresponds to historical $1$ (gamma power peaks at low channel indices, alpha/beta at high indices); `"deep_to_superficial"` corresponds to $-1$. |
| **6. Contact Spacing** | `intdist: float` in $\mathrm{mm}$ (default $0.05\,\mathrm{mm} = 50\,\mu\mathrm{m}$). Used for mid-zone width calculation. | Inter-contact pitch determines spatial resolution; crossover reported in distance ($\mu\mathrm{m}$ or $\mathrm{mm}$). | Explicit `contact_spacing: float` in $\mu\mathrm{m}$ (or derived from `ProbeGeometry.nominal_pitch`). | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Adheres to strict library-wide micrometer ($\mu\mathrm{m}$) coordinate standard. Enforces pitch uniformity (`is_uniform=True`). |
| **7. Missing & Bad Contacts** | Splices probe into contiguous clean segments; discards any segment with length $< 8$ channels. | Spatial masking or interpolation along cortical column. | Accepts optional `bad_channel_mask: np.ndarray[bool]`; uses 1D linear interpolation along depth for gradient/profile; reports missing count in result. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Prevents discarding entire probes due to a single isolated bad contact, while preserving strict rejection if valid coverage is insufficient ($< 8$ contacts). |
| **8. Profile Gradient** | Band averages: $L(c) = \text{mean}_{f \in \text{low}} \tilde{P}(c, f)$, $H(c) = \text{mean}_{f \in \text{high}} \tilde{P}(c, f)$; difference $\Delta(c) = H(c) - L(c)$. | Difference between normalized gamma power and normalized alpha/beta power along depth. | Difference profile $\Delta(c) = H(c) - L(c)$ (oriented along superficial $\to$ deep). | **IDENTICAL** | $\Delta(c)$ evaluates the local dominance of high vs low frequency oscillatory power at contact $c$. |
| **9. Crossover Estimator** | Initial zero-crossing: $\text{argmin}_c \|\Delta(c)\|$; refined within $\pm 4$ channels via score: $-\|\Delta(c)\| + 0.25 \|\nabla \Delta(c)\|$. | Zero-crossing / inflection point where standardized gamma equals standardized alpha/beta power. | Continuous zero-crossing via linear sub-contact interpolation of $\Delta(c) = 0$; optional gradient refinement. | **EQUIVALENT** | *Derivation*: Continuous interpolation computes $c^* = c_i + \frac{-\Delta(c_i)}{\Delta(c_{i+1}) - \Delta(c_i)}$, providing sub-contact spatial resolution without integer discretization error. |
| **10. Objective Function & Support Score ($\Omega$)** | Metric: $\Pi = P_{s,\text{dist}} \cdot D_{\text{band}} \cdot \|\text{goodness}\| \cdot \Delta c_{\text{peaks}} \cdot N_{\text{low}} \cdot N_{\text{high}}$; support score $\Omega = \ln(\Pi)$. | Evaluates separation between supragranular gamma peak and infragranular alpha/beta peak across contacts. | Evaluates peak separation, contrast magnitude, and transition monotonicity; computes support score $\Omega$. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Support score $\Omega$ is returned in `VFlipResult` on ALL runs, including rejected fits, ensuring diagnostic inspectability. |
| **11. Acceptance & Rejection Semantics** | `omega_cut: float = 6.0`. If $\Omega < \text{omega\_cut}$, returns `Results = None`. In tests, allowed $\Omega_{\text{cut}} = -\infty$ to force fit. | Probes lacking clear spectrolaminar motif or with low contrast are considered non-identifiable. | `min_support_score: float = 6.0`. If $\Omega < \text{min\_support\_score}$, returns `accepted=False`, `crossover_contact=None`. No $-\infty$ bypass. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Strictly conforms to jnwb frozen failure semantics (0.2.0-07): rejected fits return `accepted=False` and `None` for crossover; never fabricate plausible values. |
| **12. Layer Classification (`label_layers`)** | `get_laminar_label_vector()`: Centers mid zone of width $\lceil \text{layer4} / \text{pitch} \rceil$ on crossover; assigns `"sup"`, `"mid"`, `"deep"`, `"na"`. Hardcoded 128 channels. | Granular layer (L4) centered at crossover; superficial (L1–3) above; deep (L5–6) below. | `label_layers(vflip_result, probe_geom, *, granular_thickness_um=400.0) -> dict[Any, str]`. Assigns `"superficial"`, `"input"`, `"deep"`, `"na"`. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Dynamic channel count (no hardcoded 128); explicit $\mu\mathrm{m}$ thickness; on `accepted=False`, all channels receive `"na"`; standard anatomical terminology. |
| **13. Interactive Hooks & Display** | Contained `plot_result: bool`, matplotlib plotting logic, and historical prompt hooks. | Computational method described in paper text and figures. | Strict, headless, non-interactive functional implementation. Zero GUI/plotting side effects in core estimator. | **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGE** | Core library functions must remain headless, fast, and pure. Visualizations belong in `visual_qc` or examples. |
| **14. Determinism & Randomness** | Deterministic mathematical optimization. Zero RNG calls. | Deterministic algorithm. | Strictly deterministic. Zero RNG calls. | **IDENTICAL** | Guarantees exact reproducibility across calls and runs on identical input arrays. |

## Resolution Status

- **IDENTICAL items**: 2 (Spectral-gradient difference profile, Determinism)
- **EQUIVALENT items**: 3 (Spectral standardization across depth, Orientation mapping, Continuous crossover interpolation)
- **INTENTIONAL JNWB CHANGES**: 9 (Input decoupling, Explicit band bounds, Geometry integration, Explicit $\mu\mathrm{m}$ units, Bad-contact interpolation, Always-returned support score, Fail-loud rejection semantics, Dynamic layer labeling, Pure non-interactive execution)
- **UNRESOLVED items**: **0 (None)**.

Every consequential difference is identified, derived, or scientifically justified. The reference contract is established and frozen for implementation in `0.2.2-02`.
