- `jnwb.vis.plot_wavemap(result, waveforms)` draws a `WaveMAPResult`: the 2-d embedding coloured by cluster beside each cluster's mean normalized waveform; `layout` and `height_mm` are keyword-only. It refuses an empty result and labels, waveforms or an embedding of different lengths with `ValueError`. Tutorial 10 runs it on the DANDI 000253 excerpt, which now carries the source's mean waveforms; the source labels them volts, and the values read as microvolts.
