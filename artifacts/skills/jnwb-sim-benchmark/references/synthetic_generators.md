### Synthetic generators

- `jnwb.testing.synth_vflip2_crossover(n_channels=32, n_samples=5000, fs=1000.0, *, z0, snr=4.0, tau_s=0.03, background=True, rng=None)`: one probe whose band-power crossover is the one vFLIP2 normalizes and averages. Contact `c` has low-band (13 to 23 Hz) weight `0.5 + (c - z0) / (n_channels - 1)`, clipped to [0.05, 0.95]; the high-band (60 to 90 Hz) weight is one minus it. Returns `SynthVFlip2Receipt`, with `lfp`, `z0`, `low_weight` and `high_weight` among its fields.
  - `z0` is keyword-only and required: the contact where the two weights are equal.
  - `snr` is the planted variance over the background variance, per contact; `snr=0` gives the background alone.
  - `background=False` drops the background and keeps its draws.
- `jnwb.testing.synth_laminar_motif(n_channels=24, n_samples=5000, fs=1000.0, *, c_crossover=12.0, orientation="superficial_to_deep", pitch_um=50.0, gamma_freq=75.0, beta_freq=18.0, snr=4.0, bad_channels=None, rng=None)`: an LFP recording with a known spectrolaminar motif: gamma dominance superficial, alpha/beta dominance deep, and an exact zero crossing at `c_crossover`. Returns `SynthLaminarReceipt`, with `lfp`, `crossover_contact`, `crossover_depth_um`, `probe_geometry` and `bad_channel_mask` among its fields.
- `jnwb.testing.synth_white_noise(shape, *, scale=1.0, mean=0.0, rng=None)`: Gaussian white noise with a known scale and mean. Returns an array of `shape`.
- `jnwb.testing.synth_ar_noise(n_samples, *, n_channels=1, tau_s=None, poles=None, fs=1000.0, sigma_innov=1.0, rng=None)`: autoregressive noise with a set autocorrelation, given as an AR(1) time constant `tau_s` or as AR `poles`, not both. Returns `(n_samples,)` for one channel and `(n_channels, n_samples)` otherwise. The first samples carry less than the stationary variance when `tau_s * fs` is large against the burn-in of `max(200, 20 * p)` samples.
- `jnwb.testing.synth_periodic_response(n_trials, n_samples, fs, *, freq_hz=20.0, n_channels=1, amplitude=1.0, noise_std=0.5, phase_jitter_rad=0.0, rng=None)`: a periodic response shared across trials, with controlled trial-to-trial phase jitter. Returns `(n_trials, n_samples)` for one channel and `(n_trials, n_channels, n_samples)` otherwise.
- `jnwb.testing.synth_correlation_blocks(block_sizes, *, within_corr=0.8, between_corr=0.0, n_samples=1000, rng=None)`: multichannel series with a known block-diagonal correlation. Returns `(data, true_labels, true_corr)`.
- `jnwb.testing.synth_phase_gradient(n_channels, n_samples, fs, *, f0=25.0, delay_per_channel_s=0.002, noise_std=0.2, rng=None)`: a linear array with a known traveling-wave phase gradient. Returns `(n_channels, n_samples)`.
- `jnwb.testing.synth_unequal_groups(n_trials_1, n_trials_2, n_features, *, effect_size=0.0, noise_std=1.0, rng=None)`: two sample groups of unequal size for a comparison test. Returns `(group1, group2)`.
- `jnwb.testing.build_canonical_tutorial_nwb(output_path=None, *, n_channels=24, pitch_um=50.0, fs=1000.0, duration_s=10.0, n_trials=20, seed=42)`: a self-contained synthetic NWB file for the executable tutorials. Returns `(nwbfile, ground_truth)`.
