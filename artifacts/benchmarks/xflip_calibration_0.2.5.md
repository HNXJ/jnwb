# xFLIP Empirical Calibration & Operating Characteristics Receipt

Generated by `scripts/calibrate_xflip.py` from estimator SHA-256 `bc19ff86f3e7f704`.

This supersedes `xflip_calibration_0.2.3.md`, which had no generator and could not
be regenerated. Two changes had already invalidated it: 0.2.4 made `xflip` reject a
zero-variance channel rather than report its correlation as 0, and the smooth-gradient
drop gate turned out to be skipped on the `contiguous=False` path, so the null rates
it reported were conditional on a setting it did not name.

### Reading the null table

- `ar_noise` accepts 2 of 30 seeds (0.067), above the nominal 0.05. At this many seeds that is inside the binomial interval for a true rate of 0.05, so it is reported, not diagnosed. Correlated noise is where this estimator is hardest to calibrate, and it is the one family whose rate the surrogate test alone controls.
- `smooth_spatial_gradient` has median, min and max omnibus p all at 0.0050, the 1/(surrogates+1) floor. Every gradient is maximally significant under the permutation test, which rejects 0 of 30; the local boundary-drop gate rejects 29 of 30, and the 0.033 acceptance rate is the gradients it lets through. When that gate was skipped on the unrestricted path, gradients were accepted 15/15.
