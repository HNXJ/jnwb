# xFLIP Empirical Calibration & Operating Characteristics Receipt

Generated by `scripts/calibrate_xflip.py` from estimator SHA-256 `7f23f7a9551e5131`.

This supersedes `xflip_calibration_0.2.3.md`, which had no generator and could not
be regenerated. Two changes had already invalidated it: 0.2.4 made `xflip` reject a
zero-variance channel rather than report its correlation as 0, and 05-07 found the
smooth-gradient drop gate was skipped on the `contiguous=False` path, so the null
rates it reported were conditional on a setting it did not name.

### Reading the null table

- `ar_noise` accepts 2 of 30 seeds (0.067), above the nominal 0.05. At this many seeds that is inside the binomial interval for a true rate of 0.05, so it is reported, not diagnosed. Correlated noise is where this estimator is hardest to calibrate, and it is the one family whose rate the surrogate test alone controls.
- `smooth_spatial_gradient` has median, min and max omnibus p all at 0.0050, the 1/(surrogates+1) floor. Every gradient is maximally significant under the permutation test; the 0.000 acceptance rate is produced entirely by the local boundary-drop gate. Reading the rate without this line inverts what 05-07 established -- the permutation test does not reject gradients, and when that gate was skipped on the unrestricted path they were accepted 15/15.

## What this receipt does not cover

- Only `contiguous=True`. The unrestricted path is exercised by
  `tests/test_xflip_calibration.py::TestXFlipGradientGateOnBothPaths`, not here.
- One correlation method (`pearson`) and one surrogate method per family.
- Non-contiguous and overlapping block structure.
- Real recordings. Every family is synthetic.
