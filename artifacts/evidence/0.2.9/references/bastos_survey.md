## Table 2. Pitfalls in BastosSchoffelen2016 mapped to jnwb

| Pitfall | Paper's remedy (paraphrase) | jnwb safeguard or function | Test or doc statement needed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common reference | Bipolar derivation or a separate reference per site | `bipolar_reference`, `laplacian_reference`, `current_source_density_1d` | Test: a shared reference signal gives inflated coherence and GC on unipolar data, and the inflation drops after `bipolar_reference`. Doc: re-reference before coherence and GC (Bastos2020PNAS says the same) | observed (paper); inferred (whether a jnwb test exists; not checked) |
| Volume conduction / field spread | Source reconstruction or local re-referencing, experimental contrasts, or phase-lagged metrics; none removes it fully | `imaginary_coherency`, `wpli` (docstring already says these reduce sensitivity without conferring immunity), `phase_slope_index` | Test: zero-lag instantaneous mixing leaves ImCoh and wPLI near 0 while coherence is high. Doc: say the same for `cross_area_coherence` | observed (paper, and spectral.py:1782 wording) |
| SNR asymmetry | Power stratification by trial subsampling; time-reversed GC to separate strong from weak asymmetries | none found. `granger`/`granger_spectral` have no time-reversal option; no stratification helper | Test/doc: noise added to one channel flips or creates GC direction. Candidate: a time-reversal check in `directed_connectivity` | observed (paper); observed (grep found no time-reversal in jnwb/) |
| Common input | Record the candidate driver; partial coherence; conditional or partial GC | `granger` (docstring says "bivariate or conditional"); no partial coherence | Test: a common driver with unequal delays makes bivariate GC spurious, and conditioning removes it. Doc: bivariate `granger_spectral` cannot rule out common input | observed (paper; connectivity.py:1150) |
| Sample-size bias | Bias-free metrics (PPC, debiased wPLI), or equalize trial counts by bootstrap subsampling | `pairwise_phase_consistency`, `wpli` (`wpli_debiased_sq`), `cross_area_coherence` reports `n_segments_used` with a null near 1/K | Test: under a null, PPC and debiased wPLI have mean about 0 for every K, and coherence tracks 1/K. Doc: compare conditions only at equal K | observed (paper; spectral.py:756) |
| Phase-difference slope as direction | Using the slope alone to assign direction is unprincipled; PSI is more robust | `phase_slope_index` | Doc: PSI is temporal-lag asymmetry, not anatomy (AGENTS.md §4.8, §5) | observed (paper) |
| Parametric vs nonparametric GC | Nonparametric needs more data and smooth cross-spectra; parametric needs a model order and is more sensitive when the order is right | `granger_spectral` is parametric only: VAR with `order='auto'` by BIC; its docstring says Wilson factorization "is not implemented" | Doc: Bastos lab papers use nonparametric GC, so jnwb values are not directly comparable. Test: order sensitivity. Gap: no nonparametric GC | observed (paper; connectivity.py:1463-1468) |
| Bivariate vs multivariate decomposition | Multivariate transfer matrix; conditional GC | `granger` (conditional), `directed_network` | Doc: which network functions condition and which are pairwise | observed (paper); inferred (`directed_network` semantics not read) |
| Non-stationarity | Not discussed in this paper (0 full-text hits for "stationar") | `granger_causality` ADF warning; `granger_spectral` flags spectral radius >= 1 | none from this source; cite Friston2014GC for unstable slow modes instead | observed (keyword scan; connectivity.py:437-508, 1567) |
| Filtering before GC | Only cited (Barnett and Seth 2011), not treated | `bandpass_filter`, `notch_filter` | Doc: do not band-pass before GC. The source is Barnett and Seth 2011, not this paper | observed (citation only) |
| Spectral leakage (taper) | Not treated. "Leakage" in the paper means spatial field spread | `compute_multitaper_psd` | none from this source | observed (keyword scan) |
