# Rulings of 2026-09-26

Made by Hamm during the 0.2.7 cycle. Each ruling names the work it creates; that work is executed through `artifacts/todo_stack.md`.

| Item | Ruling | Work it creates |
|---|---|---|
| `xflip` partition objective | **The per-block sum of within-block similarity squared over its pair count**, replacing the form that subtracts the probe-wide mean from every within-block pair and so favours balanced blocks; cuts change on existing data and the calibration is regenerated. | IA-30 |
| `xflip` null calibration | **A stated rate bound at or below alpha over enough seeds** replaces the zero-count checks; the smooth-gradient null rate of about 0.02 to 0.03 under the new objective is calibrated | IA-30 |
| The surrogate-gate test of `xflip` | **Isolates that gate** by passing `min_contrast=0` and `min_boundary_drop=0`, so only the surrogate test can reject, as the test's name says | IA-30 |
| `rng=None` for scikit-learn estimators | **One int drawn from fresh entropy** in `sklearn_random_state`, so no call reads or advances NumPy's global state; a global `np.random.seed` no longer makes `rng=None` reproducible. Graded highly recommended (85) | nested_cv_linear_svm |
| The response classifier's test | **A Wilcoxon signed-rank test on per-trial rate differences**: `compute_response_metrics` adds per-trial `baseline_rates` and `response_rates` (additive); `classify_response_significance` runs it with `zero_method='wilcox'`, requires p below a new `alpha` beside the z gate, and gives `undefined` with a warning when the arrays are absent | IA-18 |
| `explained_variance` of `compute_population_trajectory` | **Keep the old scalar for this release and warn on read**; `explained_variance_ratio` and `explained_variance_per_component` carry the new values, and the key switches to the per-component meaning in the next release | IA-12 |
