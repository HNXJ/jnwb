# Rulings of 2026-10-15

Made by Hamm, on the external review of 0.2.12 and the post-release state audit. Each ruling names the work it creates.

| Item | Ruling | Work it creates |
|---|---|---|
| 15-17, xFLIP null provenance (Hamm) | Approved. `XFlipResult` gains `null_method`: the null actually executed, from a closed, documented set, with `'auto'` recording what it resolved to, including the precomputed-matrix fallback; method selection, sample-count information and warnings stay as they are; the result is extended backward-compatibly. Explicit, automatic and fallback paths are told apart by tests. An effective sample size in place of nominal `n_samples` is a separate statistical ruling with its own calibration, never a silent change | 15-17 |
| 15-18, fusion disagreement (Hamm) | Conditionally approved; no fixed distance cutoff. Both landmarks and their raw separation stay in the result; anchor quality is told apart from cross-estimator agreement; a valid primary anchor survives a secondary's disagreement; no high-confidence consensus grade while agreement is unresolved or materially poor. Separation is read in units of contact spacing ($\delta = d / s$; on irregular geometry a documented local resolution, never a global mean), and no numerical agreement threshold is chosen until calibration gives it a meaning. The smallest change is an explicit agreement status with conservative grade capping, never a new weighted confidence score without validation | 15-18 |
| Fact schedule (Hamm) | Amended: the 0.2.12 deadline has passed, and the schedule moves, not the obligations. Each of the 32 UNHELD facts gets a disposition, an owner or dependency, an evidence requirement and a target cycle: required for shipped scientific or API correctness, investigated now; required for 0.2.13 changes, assigned to that item and verified before 0.2.13; dependent on future capability or external input, moved to the earliest justified cycle with the dependency kept; duplicated or superseded, proposed for removal or replacement with evidence. UNHELD is never HELD: no rescheduling counts missing evidence as passing, and a fact whose falsity would invalidate a 0.2.12 claim is escalated, not rescheduled | `artifacts/evidence/0.2.13/fact_dispositions.md`; `standing.md` fact schedule row |
| S7 holder (Hamm) | S7 moves from `todo:11-07` to `todo:12-11` provided that 12-11 supplies the evidence S7 requires with the same or stronger acceptance; if it covers only part, the holder splits or S7 stays UNHELD under 11-07 | `artifacts/fact_stack.md` S7 row; roadmap 12-11 |
| 0.2.13 order (Hamm) | 15-11 before 15-19, by dependency; 15-17's provenance field and 15-18's grading contract are specified independently of the vFLIP2 calibration, and only their numerical thresholds wait for evidence. 15-20 inventories each gate's purpose, runtime, unique failure detection, duplicated assertions and maintenance burden before any bound is proposed, with no universal gate-count ceiling and no constraint that rewards deleting valuable tests | roadmap laminar and harness themes |
