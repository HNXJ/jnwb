- `aperiodic_fit(..., remove_peaks=True)`, keyword-only and False by default. It fits twice, as the robust aperiodic fit of FOOOF 1.1.0: the second fit keeps the bins whose residual above the first fit, clipped at 0, is at or below its 0.025th percentile, so a peak inside `freq_range` no longer biases `exponent` (a 10 Hz peak on a 1/f^2 spectrum gives 2.16 by default and 2.02 with it). No Gaussian peaks are fitted; `r_squared` is over the kept bins, and fewer than 4 kept bins reject the fit. The default fit is unchanged.
