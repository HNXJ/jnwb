- `classify_unit_quality` no longer passes a unit it cannot judge as `Good`: a missing or non-numeric value (NaN, `None`, a label such as `'mua'`) is flagged `'<metric> undefined'`, a threshold whose column is absent flags `'<metric> absent'`, and such a unit is the new class `'Unknown'` with `is_valid=False`, unless a measured `quality` or `snr` failure makes it `'Poor'`.
- `assign_quality_tier` returns `'unknown'` for a missing quality, a code other than 0 or 1, or a label, where it returned `'unstable'`; its docstring states the quality rule it shares with `enrich_units_dataframe`.
- `audit_units` takes `quality_threshold=` and `snr_threshold=` (keyword-only, default 1.0) in place of a hard-coded 1.0, reports the standard deviation of a single value as NaN rather than 0.0, and names the unit whose `spike_times` entry is not a sequence.
- `enrich_units_dataframe` takes `stable_threshold=` (default 1.0) and `stable_labels=` (default `("good", "sua", "single", "stable", "clean")`), and `get_all_units_metadata` passes `stable_labels=` through. The defaults of these, of `classify_unit_quality`, `get_snr_analysis`, `get_all_units_metadata` and `assign_quality_tier` are now documented as conventions with no cited source.
- `jnwb.metadata.compare_old_new_criteria` requires `new_key` and `old_key`, whose defaults named one downstream corpus's columns; reports a missing new class as transition `'unknown'` instead of `'gained'`, `'unchanged_excluded'` or an exception; and raises `ValueError` when a key occurs twice in either frame.
- `plot_unit_waveforms` takes `channels=` (`"peak"` or `"all"`) for `(n_channels, n_samples)` templates and `(n_spikes, n_channels, n_samples)` spikes, which it drew as a channel mean that shrank the peak by the channel count, and raises `KeyError` for a unit with no waveform instead of drawing an empty panel.
