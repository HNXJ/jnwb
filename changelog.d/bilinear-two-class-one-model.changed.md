- `jnwb.bilinear.BilinearLogisticRegression` fits one model for two classes, and `predict_proba` returns its own probability, sigmoid(D). It was the softmax of two mirrored one-vs-rest models, about sigmoid(2 D) and overconfident: on held-out simulated trials its 0.8 to 0.9 bin predicted 0.85 where 0.65 was observed, and the count-weighted gap between predicted and observed frequency falls from 0.085-0.19 to 0.02-0.09 across ten simulated populations. Two-class labels and decision values are unchanged (the mirrored models are negations of each other; they agreed to 1e-13 on six simulations), and `n_parameters()` counts the one model, half its old value. With three or more classes nothing changes.
